runway is a private running planner and training decision ledger for Android.

Build a conservative plan around your current training, available days, and goal. The calendar keeps
the prescribed run, the work you completed, rest, missed work, and the next decision together. You
can change an individual workout, record a different run, or skip a day without losing the original
plan. Any suggested plan change is shown before you choose whether to apply it.

Import runs from a GPX file, a folder you approve, or Health Connect. Every imported run waits in
Review before it can complete a planned workout or affect future training. Folder access is limited
to the directory you choose. Health Connect access is optional, read-only, and limited to running
sessions and their relevant metrics. Route access is requested separately for each run, and route
points can be discarded after import.

Plans, activities, private notes, heart-rate data, and preferences stay in runway's local app
storage. There is no account and no remote service. You control complete local backups and readable
training exports; both are plaintext files, so runway warns you before creating them.

runway is not a GPS recorder, social network, or medical coach. It helps make a training ramp,
recovery, completed work, missed work, and the next decision easier to reason about.
