Android is now runway's primary, full-featured client. This release adds native local signup,
password and two-factor sign-in, account security, onboarding, month calendar and day detail,
workout editing and results, review, progress, history, imports, and server/build status.

The interface now follows the phone's light or dark theme, makes the next run easier to find,
preserves timed prescriptions without fake distances, and explains plan changes before applying
them. OIDC, passkey sign-in, password reset, and new passkey registration remain secure
system-browser boundaries; the rest of the product stays in native Jetpack Compose.

The responsive web client remains available, but its retired PWA manifest and service-worker paths
now return 404.
